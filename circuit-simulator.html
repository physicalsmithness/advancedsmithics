Component reference & syntax

Connecting things up

,series separator (newline also works)
(A;B)parallel branches. Bracket-less trailing form :A;B drops the brackets when it's the last thing; add \ if anything follows it
/Xvoltmeter tight across X
> <chevron current marker between atoms (auto-direction with a single battery; add a number for the current value)

Sources & power

csingle cell
cb  ·  Ncb  ·  cb*battery (default 2 cells  ·  N cells  ·  unspecified-N with dots)
b  ·  Nbalias for cb  ·  Ncb
vc  ·  vb  ·  Nvbvariable cell  ·  variable battery (with diagonal arrow)
pv  ·  Npvsolar cell (photovoltaic) — incoming-photon arrows; default 0.5 V per cell
circell with internal resistance — small cell + small resistor in a dashed box; labels via args: 6V, 0.1ohm, E, r, or any combination
pdc  ·  pacDC  ·  AC power supply
trtransformer — defaults to right wall, draws its own load loop

Loads

r  ·  r10resistor (with optional Ω value)
vr  ·  vr50variable resistor / rheostat
pot  ·  pot(X)potentiometer; (X) puts component X on the wiper branch — left-trailing by default, pot!(X) for right-trailing
bb  ·  lamp  ·  lbulb (filament lamp)
th  ·  ntcthermistor
ldrlight-dependent resistor
f  ·  f5fuse (with optional A rating)
htrheater
m  ·  gmotor  ·  generator
d  ·  leddiode  ·  LED (auto-orient with a single battery)

Switches & connections

s  ·  scopen  ·  closed switch
s2  ·  s2(A;B)two-way switch — (A;B) routes to two parallel branches
wire  ·  wlabelled wire; modifiers fat, wiggle
e  ·  gndearth  ·  ground
o  ·  o.Xopen-circuit terminal — first or last makes the loop open; label via subscript

Meters

a  ·  a1  ·  a.1ammeter (reading 1 A  ·  subscript A₁)
v  ·  v6  ·  v.1voltmeter (reading 6 V  ·  subscript V₁)
a5mohm  ·  v1Mohminternal resistance (non-ideal meters)

Sound

bzelectric bell
sp  ·  lsloudspeaker
micmicrophone

Numbers, prefixes & labels

r10primary value (Ω for resistors, V for cells, …)
r10ohm  ·  bb3ohmforce ohms as the unit on any component
r10k  ·  r10M  ·  r10mSI prefixes — case-sensitive (M = mega, m = milli)
r10mu  ·  r10µ  ·  r10umicro (any spelling); meg also = M
3cb63 cells at 6 V (number before = cells, after = volts)
r10 6V 0.5Along-form value plus voltage and current annotations
r.1  ·  a.1  ·  cb.2subscript labels (R₁, A₁, B₂)
r.  ·  bb.  ·  m.just the label letter (R, L, M) with no subscript or value
r..X  ·  a..Yarbitrary label — extra dot drops the default prefix; shows just X / Y

Position & modifiers

(no suffix)bottom wall — except battery/cell/power supplies default to top
+  ·  -right  ·  left wall
++  ·  --top wall
left,at start: battery on left, components on right
!reverse direction (battery polarity  ·  diode  ·  pot wiper side)
pmshow + / − markings on cells and batteries
*dotted unspecified-N battery
